How the numbers are built

  • Travel agency – newest Travel agency bookings file, visible Air / Hotel / Rail tabs (hidden tabs are old copies). Dated by invoice date; country = destination.
    • Duplicates removed – the September extract repeats hotel stays under the hotel group name and the brand name, and repeats some air rows.
    • Currency corrected – Tokyo hotel stays one October week were in yen in the USD column; converted at 1/150 USD per yen.
  • Expense claims – approved reports, dated by report date; amount = report total in USD, so it ties to the expense system. The export holds one expense line per report: the line keeps its expense type, the rest of the report is Not itemised in export.
  • Period – calendar 2026 only (Jan–Oct). Rows dated before 2026 are left out and shown in the tie-out.
  • People – department from the cost center the expense was charged to; travellers borrow the cost center of their own expense claims. Team from the staff mapping by employee ID, else by name.
